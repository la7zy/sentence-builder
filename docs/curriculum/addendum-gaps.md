# Addendum: gaps found in the strand plans (to fold in when the sections are authored)

Audit date: 2026-09-05. Method: keyword sweep of all plans against a standard adult ESL grammar syllabus.
Tenses were the large gap and are handled by the separate `verbs-and-tenses.md` strand, which replaces
Section 4. The items below are small and belong inside existing sections; they must be added (with the
usual 6-10 sources per rule) before those sections' banks are written.

## Section 2 — Nouns, articles & noun phrases (add: quantifiers as full lessons)
| Band | Add lesson | Rule in plain words | Must cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building | Some / any / no | "some" in positives and offers, "any" in questions and negatives, "no" = not any | somebody/anything/nowhere family |
| Building | Much / many / a lot of / lots of | many with count nouns, much with non-count, a lot of with both | questions and negatives, "how much / how many" |
| Building | A few / few / a little / little | a few = some (positive), few = not enough (negative); same for little | with count vs non-count nouns |
| Connecting | All / most / some / none / each / every / both / either / neither | which take "of", singular vs plural agreement | "every day" vs "all day", "none of" agreement (singular in formal use, plural accepted) |
| Expanding | Quantifier + of + pronoun; "the majority of"; "a number of" vs "the number of" | agreement follows the head noun | formal vs informal |

## Section 7 — Adjectives, adverbs & comparison (add: named lessons for these)
| Band | Add lesson | Rule in plain words | Must cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations | Adverbs of frequency and their position | always/usually/often/sometimes/never go before the main verb, after "be" | "How often…?", "every day" at the end |
| Building | Adverbs of manner and degree | -ly adverbs describe how; very/quite/really/too before adjectives | irregular adverbs (well, fast, hard, late), "hardly" trap |
| Building | So / such / too / enough | so + adjective, such + (a) noun, too = more than wanted, enough after adjectives and before nouns | so … that, too … to |
| Connecting | Adjective order | opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose | two or three adjectives at most in practice |
| Connecting | -ed vs -ing adjectives | bored (how you feel) vs boring (what causes it) | interested/interesting, tired/tiring, confused/confusing |
