# Grammar coverage map — every piece of English grammar and usage, and where it lives

Purpose: the single checklist that proves the platform covers the whole of English grammar and
usage for adult ESL learners from complete beginner to C1. Every topic below is placed in a
section and a band. Status shows whether the current strand plans already contain it, contain it
only thinly, or were missing it; the Action column is what the section author must add. The
additions table at the end is read by the master-table builder, so the totals in
`curriculum-master.md` include them.

Scope decision (2026-09-05): the platform is grammar-based for now. The Listening & Speaking
strand is deferred (its plan file stays for later). The Writing strand is a later phase; the
grammar sections and the proofreading checkpoints are the work in scope.

Audit method: keyword sweep of `grammar-sections-1-7.md`, `grammar-sections-8-12.md` and
`verbs-and-tenses.md` against the inventory below (script kept in the build folder), then a
reading of each thin or missing topic. "Present" = taught as its own lesson; "thin" = mentioned
but not a full lesson; "missing" = not in any plan.

Sections: 1 Sentence anatomy · 2 Nouns, articles and noun phrases · 3 Pronouns and reference ·
4 Verbs and tenses (the strand in `verbs-and-tenses.md`) · 5 Agreement · 6 Modals, conditionals
and unreal forms · 7 Adjectives, adverbs and comparison · 8 Prepositions · 9 Conjunctions and
clauses · 10 Questions, negatives and reported speech · 11 Punctuation and mechanics ·
12 Cohesion, style and word choice.
