A comparative essay is an argument, not a list

Most students think the job is to list what is the same and what is different. It is not.

A and B are similar in some ways and different in others.True of any two things in the universe. It proves nothing and decides nothing.
Although A is cheaper, B is the better choice for most students, because…The similarities and differences are your evidence. The argument is what you conclude from them.
The question you must answerSo what? Why should anyone care that these two things are different in this way?

The two mistakes almost everyone makes

Online classes are cheaper, however, in-person classes offer more support.Comma splice. Two complete sentences joined by a comma. The most common error in this essay type.
Online classes are cheaper. Whereas in-person classes offer more support.Fragment. A whereas clause cannot stand alone. It can open a sentence — but only if the main clause follows: Whereas online classes are cheaper, in-person classes offer more support.

A fourth tool: prepositions

Despite, in spite of, unlike, in contrast to, compared with take a noun or an -ing form — never a whole clause.

Although it was expensive, she chose the in-person class.Use although when you need a whole clause.

Two words students mix up

on the other handon the contrary
Doesadds a second, different point — both can be truecontradicts — says the last idea was not true
ExampleThe online class is cheaper; on the other hand, it offers no speaking practice.She wasn’t bored; on the contrary, she signed up again.
In your essayYou are weighing two true options, not correcting a false claim. So on the other hand is almost always right, and on the contrary is almost always wrong.

The one that works

Although online classes are cheaper and more flexible than in-person classes, in-person classes are more effective for most students, because of the support and interaction they provide.

Four things are happening:

  • Concession — you admit the other side wins two criteria.
  • Judgment — more effective.
  • Limit — for most students, not all.
  • Grounds — because…
The formulaAlthough [what the other side wins], [your subject] is more [judgment] for [who], because [grounds].

Notice the Although clause uses Stage 2 punctuation: conjunction first, comma at the join.

The introduction — three moves

  • Context. One sentence that puts both subjects in the same world. A then-and-now contrast works almost every time.
  • Common ground. One sentence naming what they share — this is what makes the comparison fair.
  • Thesis. Stage 4, unchanged.

The conclusion — three moves

  • Say the judgment again, in new words. Not one phrase copied.
  • Answer “so what?” Recommend one option for one kind of reader.
  • Add nothing new. No new example, no new number.
The most mature move availableRefuse the false choice. “For a disciplined, independent learner, online study is a sensible choice; for everyone else, a classroom is worth the extra money.”

The balance rule

A comparative task sets two subjects. Treating one of them thinly reads as though you skipped half the task, and a teacher can mark it as an omission.

  • Same criteria for both.
  • Same order, every time.
  • Comparable space.
Quick checkCount the words you spent on each subject. Anything outside roughly 45 : 55 is worth a second look.

One thing to noticeParagraph 4 puts B first. Keeping A→B in every paragraph is the safe default — but a writer may reverse it once, on purpose, to let the winning side speak last.

Ten years ago, taking a language class meant sitting in a classroom; today the same course can be taken on a laptop at the kitchen table. Both routes lead to the same certificate, but they do not offer the same experience. Although online classes are cheaper and more flexible than in-person classes, in-person classes are more effective for most students, because of the support and interaction they provide.

The first difference is cost. An online class usually charges a low monthly fee and requires no travel, whereas an in-person class has to cover a building, an instructor, and printed materials, so the price is normally much higher. Online study is therefore the cheaper choice for a student on a tight budget. A low price, however, only matters if the class actually works.

The second difference is flexibility. An online class can be watched at midnight or on a bus, while an in-person class happens once a week at a fixed hour in a fixed room. For a student who already works, that difference decides whether the course is possible at all. Flexibility, though, asks something in return: the discipline to use it.

The most important difference is what happens when a student does not understand. In an in-person class, an instructor sees a confused face and explains the point again in a different way; online, the same student clicks next and the misunderstanding is never cleared up. For most learners this is the difference between finishing a course and abandoning it in week three.

Cheaper and more convenient are real advantages, and for a disciplined learner online classes are a sensible choice. For everyone else, sitting in a room with a teacher who can see you is still worth the extra money.
